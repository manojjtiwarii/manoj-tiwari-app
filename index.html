Tips & due dates
DUE DATE

GSTR-1 is due on the 11th and GSTR-3B on the 20th of every month. Missing either attracts late fees even with zero tax due.

COMMON MISTAKE

Claiming input tax credit without matching it to your supplier's GSTR-1 is one of the most common reasons for a GST notice.

TDS

TDS deducted in a month must be deposited by the 7th of the next month. Interest starts accruing from day one of delay.

ITR

Choosing between the old and new tax regime? Use the calculator in the Services tab before you decide — it can change your refund.

Frequently asked
What documents do I need to get started?+
Usually your PAN, Aadhaar, last filed return (if any), and bank statements or sales/purchase records for the period in question. Exact documents vary by service — this is confirmed on your free first call.
How long does a GST or ITR filing take?+
Once documents are shared, GST returns are typically filed within 2–3 working days, and income tax returns within 3–5 working days depending on complexity.
Do you work with businesses outside Kolkata?+
Yes — everything from document sharing to filing can be done remotely over WhatsApp, email, and this app, so location isn't a barrier.
Is my financial data kept confidential?+
Yes. Client records and financial data are handled confidentially and are never shared with third parties without your consent.
Can I switch to you mid-year from another accountant?+
Absolutely — this is common. Share your existing records and the current filing status, and the transition is handled smoothly from wherever you left off.
